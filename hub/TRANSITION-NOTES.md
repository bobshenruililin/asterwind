# Transition direction and acceptance

The inspiration is tactile paper-world immersion and a picture opening into another picture. It does not copy Papetura or Gorogoa assets, characters, or exact scenes. No Threepipe dependency is installed: its inspectable object-state approach informs a small explicit navigation state machine instead of a heavier renderer.

The first beat must make the studio feel occupied. The selected can moves immediately. The corridor's warm light becomes the actual opening frame, then releases it into the story without losing setup dialogue. The drink is navigation; the leadership decision stays inside The Seam.

Acceptance: one click enters, no double dispense, no hidden playback, no stuck black frame, interrupted transitions cancel, the new environment does not imply new narrative facts, playable versus preview drinks remain clear, original film media and story are untouched. Reduced-motion users get the same story without camera reveal. No unverified claims about continuous 3D or animation quality.

Pending: actual browser composition inspection, mobile label legibility, iframe focus, real screenshot timing, and interaction at phone scale. The can remains a native editable shape with paper-toned shading; further art direction can replace its finish without altering the choice model.
