# The 3:00 AM Seam — film-first revision

Original fictional scenario, not a direct adaptation of Amanda/Hannah's workbook. This treatment adapts the supplied Aragon concept; it does not claim real group events or engineering validation.

## What failed in v1
The implementation explains action over images that do not depict it. Character reference sheets substitute for performances. The choice jumps straight to a morning result, losing the consequential labour between decision and outcome. A dashboard and moral captions explain the lesson after the story should have let the audience experience it.

## Dramatic question
Can Ren make a necessary decision without letting the person whose work changes disappear from the group's account of what happened?

Both choices are defensible. The backup protects time and reliable work but leaves Julian absent from the finish. The shared repair keeps his hands involved but burdens Mara and compromises her intended demonstration. The concluding gesture addresses responsibility after deciding; it does not undo either cost.

## Spatial and visual grammar
Mara left; Julian right; Ren foreground between. Repeat the table axis. Match the lamp, sleeves and pavilion geometry. Wide shots establish relationships; close inserts show causes; faces show reactions. Never use a portrait sheet as a dramatic shot. Avoid animated zoom on every still. Hold until the action is understood, then cut.

Warmth is shared worklight, not moral approval. No evil blue branch versus good amber branch. Morning stays the same light in both outcomes. Show any time cue briefly as an editable overlay, never generated text.

### Backup branch
Ren: “Use the backup. Julian, get some sleep.” Hold three seconds. Julian: “Okay.”
New work insert: Ren and Mara fit the clean rib; Julian is softly visible asleep in background. The original rests carefully aside, not in a bin. Ren quietly: “Leave his original here.” Hold the labour for roughly ten seconds, do not fill with narration.
Blackout, one second. Brief 08:00 cue. Morning A: fan rises; canopy opens. Mara: “There.” Julian: “I missed it.” Ren: “The test? We can run it again.” Beat. Julian: “Finishing it.”

### Repair branch
Ren: “Let's splint it. Together.” Mara: “Then I need you on the canopy with me.”
Work insert: hands and sleeves show all three holding different parts; alignment, constraint, effort. Julian: “Hold it there.” Mara: “I am.” Hold roughly ten seconds; no heroic montage music.
Blackout, one second. Brief 08:00 cue. Morning B: fan rises; part catches; dry flutter; fan stops. Julian: “It held.” Beat. Mara: “Half of it.” Restraint, not spite.

### Common ending, 12–16 seconds
The phone's frame is widened to include both people and the actual model, not just a perfect object. Ren: “Let's record what happened. Both of you.” Mara checks the model and makes room. Julian reaches toward the seam: “Start here.” Cut as they begin explaining.

This is a gesture toward shared authorship and accountability, not proof that damage or resentment is solved. The actual branch-specific model must remain visible. If a common asset cannot preserve its geometry, use separate composites or hold the matching morning image rather than visually claiming both outcomes are identical.
