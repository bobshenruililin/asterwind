The Seam

Captioned animatic ·

An illustrated film study

This version explores shot composition, visual causality and one consequential action. The images are held illustrations with camera reframing, not completed character animation. Finished voice performances are not included. Captions are the default. Temp voice is an optional computer-generated timing guide, not acted dialogue. Sound is optional synthesized ambience.

The story is an original leadership scenario developed from Aragon's proposal. It is not a direct adaptation of Amanda and Hannah's workbook. Outcomes are authored possibilities, not predictions or engineering simulations.
