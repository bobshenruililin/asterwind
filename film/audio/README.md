# Temporary synthetic timing voices

These are computer-generated timing guides, not actors, finished dialogue, voice clones or a production soundtrack. Mara uses flite slt, Julian rms, Ren awb. Listening quality is not verified.

The player defaults to captions. The Temp voice control opts in. Clips follow the story timeline and pause with it. Each MP3 is keyed to a cue id; manifest.json lists exact text and duration. All durations fit their cue windows without speeding up speech. source-manifest.json preserves the rendering report.

Generated locally through FFmpeg's flite filter. No paid generation or third-party upload was used to produce them.
