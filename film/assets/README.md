# Asset organization

Playable WebP art lives directly in this directory and is embedded into the HTML. Unused v1 location/character/geometry studies are preserved under `reference/`, excluded from the export.

The new shots derive from original PNGs in the sibling `seam-cinema-v3` folder: failure insert, Mara offer, Julian hesitation, backup labour, shared splint labour, closing account. Neutral decision and branch-specific morning art derive from sibling `seam-assets-v2`.

Optimized copies do not overwrite any originals. Exact puppet/material details and mechanical geometry vary between generated compositions; these are authored story illustrations, not engineering documentation.

The mobile decision uses `decisionPortrait.webp`, derived from native tall `07-decision-portrait.png`; it is not a blurred or letterboxed reuse of the landscape image.
