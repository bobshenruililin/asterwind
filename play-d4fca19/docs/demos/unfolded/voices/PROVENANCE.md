# Voices: provenance

## scratch/

- **Made with:** Kokoro-82M ONNX (`onnx-community/Kokoro-82M-v1.0-ONNX`, quantised), Apache-2.0, run locally via `kokoro-onnx` on 6 November 2026.
- **Voices:** `bm_lewis` (Ellis, speed 0.86) and `bf_emma` (Ren, speed 0.9).
- **Processing:** leading silence removed, loudness normalised to −20 LUFS, mono MP3 at 80 kbps.
- **Purpose:** scratch only, to time the scene and to compare with the team's recordings. No paid service was used.
- **Lines:** the ten HEARD cues in `../RECORDING-BRIEF.md`. All are fictional, written for this prototype, and are proposals.
