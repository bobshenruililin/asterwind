This is an authored, still-based paper theatre. Camera drift and paper reveals move; the flat characters do not act. The castle is an existing image, not a newly built 3D model. Replaceable clip slots are empty until you add rendered video.

All-nine mode preserves all four original workbook options. The short classroom path adapts Q5 to two options (A and C), followed by one optional scene (Q3). Allow discussion time; it does not race through nine scenes in five minutes. Immediate feedback is a narrative adaptation; the workbook’s full quiz recommends feedback after all nine answers.

Reflections are not scores, diagnoses or validated measures of leadership. The workbook perspective is shown separately from proposed staging and possible consequences.

Tab / Enter: move and choose · Space: pause motion · →: next beat / reflect without choosing · 1–4: choose · R: replay. Nothing chooses for you. Reduced motion removes camera and paper movement. Options and feedback stay until you continue.
