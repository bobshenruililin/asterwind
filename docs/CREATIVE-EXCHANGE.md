## A lightweight way to contribute

Post a short PR comment, or add a dated note and link it here. Any of us can use:

- **Observation / idea:** the specific scene, moment or problem.
- **Evidence:** source link, timestamp, screenshot, demo and exact commit when relevant. Say what was actually watched or tested.
- **Proposal or counterproposal:** what might improve, and what trade-off it creates.
- **Need from the others:** one question, request or decision.
- **Status:** exploring, proposed, approved, built or verified, with who decided and when.

Keep decisions distinct from suggestions and tests distinct from creative approval. Do not overwrite another person's work to resolve a disagreement. Use additive notes and bounded branches; preserve the current six-film routes, saves and separate source-based app. Keep private chats and credentials out of this public repository.
