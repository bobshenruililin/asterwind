# From the CCGL classroom into the paper world

5 November 2026. New creative direction for discussion in our [Creative Exchange](CREATIVE-EXCHANGE.md). **An entrance and packaging proposal, not a runtime change or a replacement for Opus's current work.**

## The entrance

Begin in the actual CCGL classroom. Leave the lecture, follow the familiar HKU corridor, and discover the vending machine tucked into a believable building corner. The course connection comes from the place and the journey before any explanation.

Bob plans to supply actual classroom/corridor photos next week; they have not arrived. Use those references to establish geography, perspective, lighting and machine scale. The recent generated mood study was liked for atmosphere, but its invented setting and scale are not an approved spatial reference.

The proposed transition: familiar campus → curious discovery → convincing drink in hand → the paper world opens as the player drinks. Explore whether the wrapper unfolds, its little image becomes a scene, or the liquid catches a paper landscape. These are alternatives to test, not three effects to pile together. Keep the current playable entrance intact while a small candidate is reviewed.

## Bottles that reward curiosity

The drink should look plausibly manufactured: coherent bottle proportions, wrapper material, label seam, ingredients panel and readable small print. Then one detail is delightfully impossible.

- A short **“Drink when…”** hook invites a feeling or predicament without naming the scenario or predicting its outcome.
- Ordinary ingredients can sit beside playful ideas such as communication or courage.
- Example copy, **proposed**: “Water, sugar, a difficult conversation, and a pinch of courage.”
- Let a turning bottle reveal the back label as optional curiosity, not a mandatory reading gate.

Keep the final label lettering editable and typeset over the artwork rather than relying on generated tiny text. Use fictional branding; no official institutional endorsement is implied. New visible names remain proposals, and do not change episode IDs, outcomes or saves. Keep meaningful accessible names and a clear entry action even when the front label is mysterious.

Useful reference views later: the classroom exit, the route to the corner, the corner straight on and from a second angle, with a doorway or another ordinary size cue. Keep identifiable bystanders, student names and private classroom material out of public assets unless separately cleared.
