# Asterwind voice audition

Checked 6 November 2026. Scope: English, restrained cinematic dialogue, rendered before playback. This is a research and integration plan, not a listening verdict. No accounts, payments, clones or new audio were created, and no script or recording was submitted to a provider.

## Recommendation

Keep the team's own performances in the audition. Compare them with the existing Kokoro scratch and **Eleven v4**, then add **Inworld Realtime TTS-2** for an inexpensive challenger or **Fish Drama 3** for detailed scene direction. Try **Qwen3-TTS 1.7B CustomVoice** locally if suitable hardware is already available. Do not integrate every provider or replace the cast before hearing the scene.

The target is a believable person trying to say something difficult, not maximal emotion. A technically clean, expressive voice can still flatten the subtext. **HEARD already provides a small, useful test:** two characters, ten lines, a cut-off, a half-laugh, uncomfortable silence and both consequences. Use the existing [recording brief](demos/unfolded/RECORDING-BRIEF.md), not a new demonstration script.

### What supports this shortlist

The independent [Artificial Analysis provider-voice arena](https://artificialanalysis.ai/text-to-speech/leaderboard/provider-voice) lists Eleven v4 first on the checked date, with overlapping uncertainty intervals among the top models. It also includes Sonic 3.6, Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS, Drama 3 and Realtime TTS-2. This compares selected native voices and listener preference, not our character casting, direction-following, editing effort or emotional truth. Its displayed pricing also differs from current provider pages in places. Use it to select auditions, not to declare a winner or budget a purchase.

### Local/open-weight choices

- **Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-1.7B-CustomVoice:** the best starting fit *for this local trial plan*, not a measured quality winner. It offers nine preset timbres, English among ten languages and natural-language delivery instructions, without supplying anyone's recording. Code and the model card list Apache-2.0. The 0.6B CustomVoice entry does **not** list instruction control, so it is not an equivalent acting test. No per-call vendor charge when self-hosted, but hardware, electricity and setup cost remain. Check the actual machine, pin model/package revisions and time a short render before committing. [Official repository](https://github.com/QwenLM/Qwen3-TTS), [model/license](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-1.7B-CustomVoice).
- **Kokoro-82M:** retain the existing scratch as a cheap, reproducible baseline. Its current role and provenance are documented [here](demos/played/PROVENANCE.md); do not mistake a timing voice for the final cast.
- **Chatterbox:** MIT-licensed, with English Turbo paralinguistic tags and other variants. The official repository now states it has been retired/unmaintained since August 2026. Useful as a bounded local experiment, not a new supported production dependency. [Repository and status](https://github.com/resemble-ai/chatterbox).
- **Fish S2-Pro open weights:** research/noncommercial license, with separate commercial permission required. “Open weights” is not a permissive commercial license. Do not ship it based on the repository headline. [Official model and license](https://huggingface.co/fishaudio/s2-pro).

### Avoid stale recommendations

- Hume's old [Octave overview](https://dev.hume.ai/voice/docs/text-to-speech-tts/overview) and [pricing](https://www.hume.ai/pricing) redirected to its Audio Decisions/evaluation offering when opened. Do not assume old Octave pricing or availability.
- OpenAI's [deprecation schedule](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/deprecations) now lists `tts-1`, `tts-1-hd` and `gpt-4o-mini-tts` for shutdown on **6 January 2027**. They are not sensible new dependencies for this file-rendering workflow.
- Qwen's paid **Qwen-Audio-3.1-TTS-Plus** and the Apache-licensed **Qwen3-TTS** checkpoint above are different products. A leaderboard result for one is not evidence for the other.

## A bounded, auditable audition

1. **Lock the ten HEARD texts and two character intentions.** Use all existing cue IDs, including both endings. Keep underplaying, interruption and silence from the recording brief. Do not turn Ellis's uncertainty into a sobbing performance or Ren into a motivational narrator.
2. **Record the human baseline first.** Two willing performers, three takes per line, plus room tone. Keep lossless originals untouched. Consent to recording/use does not authorize cloning, training or uploading to an AI provider.
3. **Select no more than two paid challengers.** Start with Eleven v4 plus Inworld TTS-2 or Drama 3. Use authorized stock or designed fictional voices, not celebrity imitations or unverified community clones. Obtain the specific account/payment/script-sharing permissions before using a service. The research itself grants none.
4. **Give each system the same opportunity.** One casting pass and three takes per cue or short scene. Keep every take and count failures. For supported multi-speaker models, test one whole-scene render too; this may improve conversational flow, but it must still cut cleanly into cues. Never train one candidate on another provider's outputs.
5. **Blind, level-matched listening.** Label takes A/B/C, match perceived loudness using the existing brief's target, and play in the actual scene with the same ambience. Ask 3–5 listeners to rate believable intention, emotional change, character consistency and exact wording on a 1–5 scale. Ask what Ellis wanted and whether Ren's promise changed that. Reveal providers afterward. This is a design test, not a statistically established benchmark.
6. **Measure usable output, not demo highlights.** Record first-take success, accepted cues/attempts, editing minutes, duration fit, mispronunciations, added/omitted words and total billed units. Stop a candidate after repeated failures on the interruption or quiet decision. Reject any otherwise attractive take that changes the script or speaker identity.
7. **Choose at scene level.** Prefer the human set when it carries the scene better; use AI for a whole approved character or bounded missing cue only if continuity survives listening. Keep captions and the silent fallback. Do not select a provider purely from a score or price.

Planning example, not a spending authorization: cap an initial provider audition at **20,000 billable characters/bytes**, including directions and retakes. At the displayed rates, raw synthesis would be $1.60 for Eleven v4, $0.50 for Inworld TTS-2, or $0.20 promotional/$1.00 standard for Drama 3. Subscription charges, minimum top-ups, tax and failed/extra attempts can change the checkout total. UTF-8 bytes and characters are not interchangeable outside ASCII text; token-billed Gemini needs its own estimate.

## Integration handoff

The inspected baseline was `833b5f1`. This branch changes documentation only. Recheck the target branch before implementation.

- **Begin in HEARD:** `docs/demos/unfolded/voices/index.json` already defines `team` and `scratch`; the `team` cue map is empty. Add a separate audition set and directory, preserving the scratch assets and cue IDs. `Snd` in `docs/demos/unfolded/index.html` loads files by set/cue, cycles sets and cancels stale playback. No provider SDK or key belongs in the static browser build.
- **Render offline:** save dry lossless masters outside the public deployment; export browser-compatible cue files, register their filenames, and keep room tone/music separate. Preserve breaths and intentional pauses. Do not silently time-stretch a performance to fit an old window; explicitly adjust the authored beat timing if approved.
- **Do not assume one manifest covers every player:** `docs/demos/played/quiet.js` and `scene.js` currently load fixed paths in `played/voices/`; The Seam uses `film/audio/manifest.json` through `seam-adapter.js`. Replacing HEARD's set will not replace those voices. Extend selection explicitly if the audition graduates to those films.
- **Keep provenance:** cue, exact text, character, model/checkpoint revision, voice ID, direction, take/seed, generation date, billed units, source/processed filenames, checksum, duration, edit history, license link/version and reviewer decision. Keep credentials, private consent records and raw personal recordings out of this public repository. Public credits should contain only the agreed credit and necessary license notice.
- **Rights gate before publication:** check script rights, commercial output rights for the actual plan, stock-voice licensing, actor consent and any attribution/disclosure requirements. An Apache/MIT software license is not permission to imitate a real person. If voice conversion/cloning is later considered, obtain separate, specific consent for the provider, purpose, retention and permitted reuse first.
- **Verification after audio integration:** run `node docs/demos/unfolded/qa/audio-lifecycle.cjs`, `node docs/demos/unfolded/qa/audio-browser.cjs <base>`, `python3 scripts/test-play-build.py` and `python3 scripts/play_assets.py`, plus checks required by the current handoff. Listen to both endings, switch/mute/pause during a cue, leave/re-enter, retry after a failed load, and verify captions and offline export. Passing file checks cannot certify emotional quality.
