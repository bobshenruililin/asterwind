# Baseline

[Back to the collection](README.md)

Two starting points: believable attention between people, and an inner world made physically visible.

These are exploratory stills. Each intent describes the idea, not a validated audience interpretation.

## B01 · The Shared Roof

![Two makers exchange a clamp above a delicate paper repair, with a vast translucent communal roof and working harbor behind them.](originals/baseline/b01-the-shared-roof.png)

**Intent:** Care and cooperation become visible through the small tool offered beneath a roof everyone depends on.

**Develop it:** Let the offered clamp carry the relationship before showing the scale of the shared roof. Can a refusal remain caring?

## B02 · The Room Behind the Answer

![A composed woman sits with a cup while her layered paper silhouette opens into a private blue room, where a smaller version of her holds a cracked vessel.](originals/baseline/b02-the-room-behind-the-answer.png)

**Intent:** A calm answer may hold an unseen inner life; attention begins by allowing that complexity.

**Develop it:** The inner room belongs to its person. What gesture lets them decide whether anyone else may enter?
