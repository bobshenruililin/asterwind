# Provenance and verification

[Back to the collection](README.md)

## What these files are

The collection contains 21 individually generated AI concept images for Asterwind, plus two earlier baseline concepts. All figures and settings are imaginative artwork. The paper texture is a generated visual treatment; these are not photographs of physical handmade miniatures.

- **M01–M07:** the source collection retained a title, symbolic intent, concise creative brief and visual-review note. Exact generation prompts were not available.
- **C01–C07 and W01–W07:** exact prompt strings were retained and are reproduced in the individual prompt records.
- **B01–B02:** the two earlier baseline PNGs were supplied for reference. Original prompts, generator version and generation logs were not available. Their short briefs are publication-time descriptions of the visible artwork.

The recorded generation tool for the 21 new images was the built-in image generation tool. A specific underlying model version, seed and exact generation timestamps are not asserted. Source manifests report visual review of the new outputs; publication checks include decoding every PNG, checking dimensions and original-byte identity, and directly reviewing both baselines and a representative set of new images. This is not a continuity, animation or audience-comprehension test.

## Traceable identity

Every entry in manifest.json has a stable collection ID, original filename, repository path, dimensions, byte count, SHA-256 and Git blob SHA-1. Those hashes identify the published bytes without exposing private storage identifiers. Repeated titles do not imply duplicate images. M01 and W06 are separate Listening Tide concepts.

No original was resized, recolored, composited or re-encoded. PNG text metadata was inspected and none was present. All 23 original images decoded successfully at 1672 × 941 pixels. The manifest excludes workspace paths, private chat extracts, private file-service IDs and unrelated documents.

## What remains unproven

- Prompt descriptions are intentions. The actual still may interpret them differently.
- Similar-looking figures across separate generations are not a verified continuity set.
- A good still is not evidence of viable motion, rigging, readable interaction, accessible alternatives or sound design.
- These concepts are not selected production assets, a story rewrite, a license grant or a claim of third-party rights clearance.
