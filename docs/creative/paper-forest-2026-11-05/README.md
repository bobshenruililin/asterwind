# Asterwind: a paper forest

**23 original PNGs: 21 new concept stills and two baseline references.**

A forest of possibilities for Bob, Robert and Opus. The collection begins with a small human gesture and asks how a world made of paper could reveal its meaning. It is a creative shelf, available to challenge, combine, redraw or leave behind.

## Five useful starting points

1. **B01 · The Shared Roof:** a tool passing between two hands gives the enormous set a human reason to exist.
2. **B02 · The Room Behind the Answer:** the material world can hold an interior feeling without an explanatory panel.
3. **M01 · The Listening Tide:** companionship shelters one small fold while the larger sea remains unsettled.
4. **C01 · The Weight of One Small Turn:** one small control connects a person to distant consequences. Develop the needs on both sides before choosing a story.
5. **W06 · The Listening Tide:** compare the intimate harbor scene with this city-scale amphitheater. How much wonder can the human exchange carry?

These are curatorial starting points, not a ranking or approval. **M01 and W06 deliberately retain the same original title; their IDs distinguish two different images.**

## Working boundaries

All 23 are exploratory references. Existing approved art, story causality, characters, routes and saves remain intact. Nothing here is wired into the app, exported runtime, main branch or deployment. New story proposals belong in their own reviewable experiment.

Full-resolution PNG originals are kept exactly once, without lossy conversion or duplicate contact sheets: 72,119,279 bytes (about 68.8 MiB) total. Each gallery page loads its own group. Use individual PNG links when bandwidth matters. No Git LFS configuration, package, external service or runtime dependency is added.

This shelf was assembled on 5 November 2026 on a separate creative branch from the shared handoff checkpoint. Publication makes the work available; it does not claim Opus has opened or approved it.
