A silent paper-theatre study

A word is only the beginning.

This is an original, silent paper-theatre prototype for Asterwind. The same “Leadership” lettering becomes a notebook heading, then a question the player has to live with.

The classroom, stairs and corridor are deliberately invented placeholders. They do not depict HKU or a verified campus route. Real photographs can replace these sets after they arrive.

The optional doorway scene is an original concept, not a source-workbook question or a validated leadership assessment. Its two consequences are authored possibilities. There are no scores or correct-answer signals.

Keyboard: Tab and Enter activate controls. Space pauses or resumes. Left / Right move between shots. R replays. At the doorway, 1 holds the door and 2 takes the other end. Escape closes this note.

Still shots: respects your device’s reduced-motion preference and advances only when you choose Next. No audio, accounts, network requests, tracking or saved progress.
