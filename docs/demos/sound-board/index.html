For listening

Sound board

Alternatives for the paper film's sound, to compare by ear. I can render and measure audio but can't hear it, so your ears decide. Every file is properly licensed and listed with its source. Nothing here is adopted, and no paid service is used.

To hear a choice in the scene, open the paper film with ?voice=kokoro-a, ?voice=kokoro-b or ?voice=piper, plus &music=sketch or &music=km-gymnopedie-no-1. Then switch on Temp voices. For example: the paper film with Kokoro A and the sketch.

1 · Voices

The same approved lines in four voices. My recommendation is still people: scratch recordings by teammates, on a phone, will carry breath and hesitation that no engine does. Of the engines, Kokoro is the most natural I can run locally under an open licence.

I excluded voices whose licences are non-commercial or share-alike. Paid options (ElevenLabs, OpenAI, Azure, Google) would need your approval before any account or spending.

2 · Recorded textures

Real paper, tape, switch, fan, rain and birds, in place of synthesis. These come only from Wikimedia Commons under public domain, CC0 or CC BY, with author and licence listed. The best source is still us: a 30-minute phone foley session with real card and kraft tape, a desk-lamp switch, a fan and the actual corridor, which gives us sound we own outright.
