Monday
The idea. Leadership as fifteen small moves instead of one big choice, in a tiny pixel studio. Everyone has a need you can’t see: the same thank-you that lifts Kit drains Asha. Nothing tells you which move is right. You find out on Friday, and the ending says which of your habits suited which person. It’s also the honest pixel-art test: hand-placed pixels, every frame under our control.
