The showcase poster · due when the bell goes

There is one good marker, and it’s in your hand.

Four parts to finish in a minute. Click a part of the poster to work on it yourself, or click a teammate to hand them the marker. Everyone is fastest at their own thing.

The idea. Control as a physical object. The marker starts in your hand, and you’re decent at everything, so holding on feels productive. The game measures what holding on costs: the parts only someone else can do quickly, the people who stop leaning in while you do their bit, and the trust lost each time you take it back.
