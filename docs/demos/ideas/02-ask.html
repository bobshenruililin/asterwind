5:32

The student repair café · 5:32, raining · the hall locks at six

Sam walks in with a split bag. You have three questions’ worth of time.

Three repairs are promised, one of them an umbrella collected at 5:50. Each question takes two minutes. Ask, then decide.

The idea. In the game, both options arrive on a plate. Real leaders decide with whatever they bothered to find out. Here, asking is an action that costs time, and the best options only exist if you asked the right people. The lesson isn’t which option is right; it’s that you can see afterwards exactly what you didn’t know.
