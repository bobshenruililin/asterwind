The morning after

The people you led leave you notes.

Not a score. What it was like on the other end of your choices.

The idea. The game ends with what you thought: your word after Leadership is…. Leadership is mostly reviewed by the people you led, in notes they’ll never send. Here they write them: a thank-you and a complaint can sit side by side on the same board, from the same night. If you’ve played the game in this browser, your real choices are used.
