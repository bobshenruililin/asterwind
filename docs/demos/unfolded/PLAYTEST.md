# Playtest kit: Asterwind · Leadership

For Bob to send to teammates and friends with the link. Copy the box below as it is; the rest of this page is for us.

---

> **Asterwind · Leadership** is a short night in paper: a notebook, a corridor after hours, six bottles, six small rooms and the films behind them. It plays in a browser, best on a laptop with sound. On a phone, turn it sideways.
>
> - **About 20 minutes** if you stay with the night. **35–45 minutes** if you follow every room into its film. You can stop any time: it remembers where you were (in your browser only).
> - **There's no score and no right answer.** Nothing you choose or write leaves your screen.
> - **Afterwards, please tell me, in a sentence or two each:**
>   1. Which moment stayed with you?
>   2. Was there a choice you hesitated over? What made it hard?
>   3. Did anything feel like it was telling you the "right" answer?
>   4. Where were you confused, or bored?
>   5. Did the rooms and the films feel like one world, or separate things?
>   6. What would you say leadership is, now? (Your own word is fine.)

---

## What we're watching for

- **Hesitation** at the choices is good: it means both sides held.
- **Whether players open the optional looks** (the folded route, Asha's notebook, Julian's hand…) and whether that changes the choice.
- **Whether *See the other way*** is used to reflect or to grade.
- **Where people stop.** The notebook (finding words) and the first film are the likely drop-offs.
- **Whether the mirror's memory line** ("You said you'd say it… Then you turned the table, and Ellis said it.") lands as recognition or feels like a verdict.
- **The ending:** what do people write after *Leadership is…*?

## Good to know before sending

- **Voices are scratch voices** (open-licence text-to-speech) until the team records its own. The **Voices** button turns them off.
- **The rooms' art is code-cut paper**, a working stand-in. The corridor, the drinks and the films are the painted art.
- **Accessibility:** keyboard-only works, and so do reduced motion and phones.
- **Not yet tried:** a real screen reader.
- **Progress is kept** in the browser's local storage. *Begin again* on the title clears it.
