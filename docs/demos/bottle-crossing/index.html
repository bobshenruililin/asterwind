Bottle crossing

One bottle, plausibly manufactured, with one impossible detail. Turn it to read the back. Hold it up to the light to cross into the paper world. Placeholder corridor and studio; all copy is proposed.

What this proof asks

  • Believable: amber glass, 330 ml, a paper wrapper with a glued seam, an ingredients panel and small print, all typeset in code so the copy stays editable.
  • The one impossible detail: the label's little window looks into the coffee, and in it, faint and upside down, a lamp-lit room.
  • The crossing: hold it up to the light and the bottle becomes a camera obscura. The room brightens, rights itself and becomes the first frame of the film.
  • Placeholders: the paper corridor stands in for the real HKU corner photos, and the studio opening plate for K-01's first frame. Proposed copy: the hook, ingredients and small print.
