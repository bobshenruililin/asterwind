What the shadows say

The Seam as a cut-paper film. The lamp throws everyone's shadow on the wall, and the shadows show what nobody says. Pick something up and do it with your own hands. Approved lines only; everything is cut in code and will be replaced by Robert's frames.

The Seam

Mara’s pre-cut spare rib. Pick it up. A strip of kraft tape for splinting Julian’s original rib. Pick it up.

What it tests. Whether paper can bend reality to show feeling, cost and what goes unsaid. The tools: cut-card figures with jointed poses, independent shadow puppets on the wall, one real action done by the player's hands, and a consequence you can see in light and shadow. Frames are stepped at 12 fps with a small hand-photographed wobble.

Not yet: Robert's painted frames, recorded sound, real voices, a phone-portrait staging.
