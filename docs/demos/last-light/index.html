The harbor could not load.

A light, not a test.

Watch the boat arrive. Then touch a place for the lantern, or carry it there. Either choice lets the story continue.

Keyboard: Tab to a choice, Enter to place the light. Space pauses. Escape cancels a drag or closes these notes.
